# Competitive and adjacent landscape for AYTO (city taste → creative decisions)

Scope: tools that measure audience demand, social trends, fashion trends, taste affinity, and generative "idea" tools, as of 28 October 2026. Older facts are dated inline. Prices are list or reported prices; most enterprise vendors quote privately. All URLs were checked in this session; where a vendor page was unreachable (403/404) and a figure comes from search snippets or third parties, this is flagged.

## Key Question 1: For each tool — what it does, who pays, pricing, and what signal it uses

### Takeaway
Almost every incumbent sells one of three things: (a) demand/viewing measurement for titles that already exist, at country level, to studios and streamers at private enterprise prices; (b) trend dashboards (social, search, retail) to marketers at $0–$600/month self-serve or $20k–$50k+/year enterprise; or (c) text/design generation with no audience data at all. Qloo is the only vendor whose core product is cross-domain *taste affinity by location*, and it sells it as an API/data layer ("Book a Demo", no public price), not as a creative tool.

### Cited Findings

- DEMAND360LITE launched 8 July 2020: "global, regional, and market-specific" analyses, 90,000+ talent and 65k+ TV shows / 60k+ movies ranked in 50+ markets — [Parrot press release](https://www.parrotanalytics.com/press/parrot-analytics-launches-data-subscription-service)

**Qloo — API capabilities (primary docs)**
- `/v2/insights` is the main endpoint; `filter.type` required; entity types: artist, book, brand, destination, movie, person, place, podcast, tv_show, video_game, plus `urn:heatmap` and partial `urn:entity:locality` — [Hackathon developer guide](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/qloo-llm-hackathon-developer-guide); [Insights API deep dive](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/insights-api-deep-dive)
- Location signals/filters: `signal.location` (WKT POINT/POLYGON or locality ID), `signal.location.query`, `signal.location.radius`, `filter.location.geohash` (12-char prefix), `filter.location.metro`, `filter.location.radius` (default 15000 m in schema vs "lesser of 10% of diameter or 2,414 m" in text); heatmap output via `filter.type=urn:heatmap` with `output.heatmap.boundary` = geohashes / city / neighborhood and `<dim>_affinity_rank` (0–1) and `<dim>_affinity` (percent rank); `bias.trends`; `feature.explainability` for per-input contribution scores; `take` max 50 — [Insights API deep dive](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/insights-api-deep-dive)
- Affinity is "quantified on a scale from 0 to 1" as the strength of correlation between an input (demographic profile, interest, or location) and an entity; the docs do not define a baseline population (search snippet; the resource URL redirected to the homepage when fetched) — [Qloo affinity explainer](https://www.qloo.com/resources/how-qloos-affinity-score-measures-consumer-preferences); popularity is a 0–1 percentile — [Insights API deep dive](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/insights-api-deep-dive)
- Qloo's location-solutions marketing (search snippet; page 404'd on fetch) says it can "layer in a city to surface local lift vs. the US baseline" and "surface the cities or regions to prioritize" — [Qloo location solutions (snippet)](https://www.qloo.com/solutions/location-geographic-analysis)

## Key Question 2: Which tools can answer "what does Hyderabad like more than the rest of India, inside crime films" or "which flavour is loved here but under-made"?

### Takeaway
Only Qloo exposes the primitives needed (location signal at city/geohash/metro granularity, affinity across films/TV/music/places/brands, and a heatmap output); every other tool is either country-level, title-level (measures demand for things that already exist), single-platform, or survey-based. None of them, Qloo included, ships a "loved here but under-made" gap metric; that must be computed on top.

### Cited Findings
- Qloo's Insights API accepts `signal.location` as a WKT point/polygon or locality, `filter.location.geohash`, `filter.location.metro`, and returns `urn:heatmap` with city/neighbourhood boundaries and affinity rank — [Insights API deep dive](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/insights-api-deep-dive); Locality Intelligence reads "the cultural shape of a place, from a block to a country" with precision "down to hundreds of feet" — [qloo.com](https://www.qloo.com/)
- Qloo markets "local lift vs. the US baseline" — the baseline it cites is the US (snippet) — [Qloo location solutions](https://www.qloo.com/solutions/location-geographic-analysis)
- Parrot's unit is demand per *market* (country); "Demand Expressions per capita" is how it compares markets; coverage 100+ markets — [Parrot country page](https://tv.parrotanalytics.com/AR/?hl=en); [Parrot help center](https://helpcenter.parrotanalytics.com/en/articles/3787220-what-is-demand)
- Ormax works from survey samples (15,600 respondents for the national OTT report) and campaign tracking per language, not city affinity — [afaqs](https://afaqs.com/news/media/indias-ott-audience-hits-601-million-paid-subscriptions-at-148-million-ormax-report-10469442)
- Cinelytic's audience module offers "demographic profiling for US audience discovery" — [cinelytic.com](https://www.cinelytic.com/); Vault predicts a given title's audience and "market white space" at genre level, not city level — [vault-ai.com](https://www.vault-ai.com/)
- GWI: 50+ markets (country-level survey) — [GWI plans](https://www.gwi.com/pricing/plans); Pinterest Trends: region/country only — [Pinterest help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-trends); TikTok Creative Center: "by region and industry" — [TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/trend-discovery?lang=en)
- Google Trends can go to city level for a *search term*, but metros are US-only and scores are relative indices, not category affinities — [Google News Initiative](https://newsinitiative.withgoogle.com/vi/resources/trainings/data-journalism/google-trends-improving-search-results)
- Spotter Studio ideates from the creator's *own* catalog and is region-restricted (US/CA/UK/AU/EU per a competitor review) — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240903095771/en); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/spotter-studio-review/)
- Foursquare sells POI search/details, not cross-category taste — [Foursquare pricing](https://foursquare.com/products/pricing/)

### Inferences
- "Hyderabad > rest of India, inside crime films" requires (1) a city-scoped affinity read, (2) a national baseline, (3) a within-category filter. Qloo supplies (1) and (3) natively (`signal.location` + `filter.type=urn:entity:movie` + `filter.tags`); (2) must be computed by the caller by running the same query at country scope and differencing, since Qloo's documented affinity has no stated baseline and its marketing baseline is the US.
- "Loved but under-made" requires a supply-side count (how many local titles exist with that flavour) that no listed tool provides; Parrot/Luminate/Ormax catalogue existing titles, which could serve as the supply denominator but are enterprise-gated.
- Google Trends is the only free tool with Indian city granularity, but it measures search volume for a term, not cross-domain taste, so it cannot answer the flavour question.

### Gaps
- Whether Qloo's localities include Indian tier-2 cities such as Tiruppur, and how dense its Indian signal is, is undocumented.
- Qloo's affinity normalization (whether a location-signalled affinity is already relative to global or national priors) is not stated in public docs.

## Key Question 3: Which products pair data with generated creative output? Is anyone doing "data finds the gap, AI writes into it"?

### Takeaway
A few products pair platform data with generation, but all are single-platform and self-referential (the creator's own channel or own script): vidIQ and Spotter Studio (YouTube), Chartmetric Flow AI (music), Jasper "Audiences" (typed personas). The film-AI cohort scores scripts but does not write; Qloo grounds other companies' generative experiences (Amex/OpenAI) but produces structured data, not creative. The only places where "Qloo finds, LLM writes" exists are Qloo's own hackathon entries.

### Cited Findings

- Jasper's "Audiences" are personas turned into AI inputs; no external market data source is named — [jasper.ai/pricing](https://www.jasper.ai/pricing)

### Gaps

## Key Question 5: Where is the clear open space for AYTO?

### Takeaway
Positioning sentence: **AYTO is the only tool that starts from a place and a format, reads what that city loves more than its own country across films, series, music, places and brands, scores which of those flavours are under-made, and writes the idea into that gap — at a creator price, not a studio price.** Everything else either measures demand for titles that already exist at country level, scores a script the creator already wrote, forecasts global seasonal trends for enterprise budgets, or generates copy with no taste data at all.

### Cited Findings (evidence behind each clause)
- "starts from a place": Qloo is the only vendor with documented city/geohash/metro location signals and heatmaps across entertainment, places and brands — [Insights API deep dive](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/insights-api-deep-dive); competitors are country-level (Parrot 100+ markets — [Parrot](https://tv.parrotanalytics.com/AR/?hl=en); GWI 50+ markets — [GWI](https://www.gwi.com/pricing/plans); Pinterest region — [Pinterest](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-trends))
- "more than its own country": Qloo's own lift framing is "vs. the US baseline" — [Qloo location solutions (snippet)](https://www.qloo.com/solutions/location-geographic-analysis); no tool found that computes city-vs-nation lift for India.
- "across films, series, music, places and brands": Qloo entity types movie, tv_show, artist, place, brand, book, podcast, destination, video_game — [Hackathon developer guide](https://docs.qloo.com/reference/qloo-llm-hackathon-developer-guide); single-domain rivals: Chartmetric (music) — [Chartmetric](https://chartmetric.com/pricing), vidIQ/Spotter (YouTube) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/plans), Exolyt/Trendpop (TikTok) — [Exolyt](https://exolyt.com/pricing)
- "under-made": Vault is the only vendor that names "market white space," and only at genre/macro level with no city input — [vault-ai.com](https://www.vault-ai.com/)
- "writes the idea": data vendors produce dashboards/predictions (Parrot, Ormax, Cinelytic, Vault, Qloo Insights); writing tools have no taste data (Jasper — [jasper.ai](https://www.jasper.ai/pricing); Sudowrite — [sudowrite.com](https://www.sudowrite.com/pricing); Final Draft has no generative AI — [Final Draft blog](https://www.finaldraft.com/blog/final-draft-13-is-here))
- "creator price": enterprise tools are demo-gated (Qloo, Parrot, Cinelytic, Vault, WGSN "tens of thousands") while prosumer tools sit at $10–$50/mo (Sudowrite $10–44 — [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/pricing); Spotter $49 — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240903095771/en); vidIQ $16.58–39 — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/plans))
- Qloo is actively inviting exactly this kind of build: "Agents, but with taste," $25k pool, Oct 30 2026 deadline — [Devpost](https://qloo.devpost.com/)

### Inferences
- What is genuinely new in AYTO is the *chain* (place+format → city-over-country lift → gap score → written creative), not the data. Honest framing: "Built on Qloo's taste graph; the lift-vs-country and gap scoring and the writing are ours."
- What is not new and should not be claimed: location-aware taste affinity (Qloo's Locality Intelligence), LLM grounding on Qloo (Amex/OpenAI, Reddit), and "data-backed ideas" (vidIQ, Spotter already use that phrase for YouTube).
- Nearest future threats: Qloo's own Insights by Qloo adding generation; Vault adding city inputs; Spotter/vidIQ adding cross-domain taste. None of these are announced.
- Commercial dependency: AYTO's hackathon key expires after judging; production needs a Qloo commercial agreement whose price is unpublished — a real go-to-market risk that competitors with public pricing (Chartmetric, Exolyt, Foursquare) do not have.

### Gaps
- No evidence either way on Qloo's Indian locality density (Hyderabad, Tiruppur), so the "Hyderabad > India" demo claim should be shown with live outputs rather than asserted.
- Qloo commercial API pricing is unknown, so AYTO's unit economics at a creator price point cannot yet be modelled from public sources.
