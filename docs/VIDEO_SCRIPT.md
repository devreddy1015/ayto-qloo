# AYTO: the 60-second video

A brief for the animators. One minute, 16:9, 1920×1080, English voiceover with burned-in captions (judges may watch muted).
Devpost doesn't require a video, but judges may score on the text, images and video alone, so this is the pitch.

**The one idea to land:** a city loves some things and isn't getting them. Qloo can see that, and a plain AI can't.
AYTO finds that gap and writes your film into it.

Every number and line on screen comes from the live site on 31 Oct 2026: a crime short film in London, written by Claude
Sonnet 5.5, and a Paris streetwear drop. Don't round or change them. The live site rewrites its showcases on each deploy,
so take the text from this file, not from a fresh run.

---

## Look and sound

- **Palette** (the app's own tokens):
  - background `#0b0b0d`;
  - text `#f5f5f7`, soft text `#b6b6be`;
  - accent yellow `#f2be22`, deeper yellow `#d9a70f`;
  - lines are white at 10% opacity.

  The yellow means only one thing: **the gap**. Use it for lanes, the open slot and the logo dot. Nothing else is yellow.
- **Type:** Archivo (Google Fonts), one width, weights 400, 600 and 700 only.
- **Shapes:** rounded cards (18 px radius), thin borders, dark glass. Calm and precise, like an instrument rather than an ad.
- **Logo:** a white ring, open at the top right, with a yellow dot in the opening (`public/icon.svg`).
  - The ring is what a city already loves.
  - The opening is what it isn't getting.
  - The dot is the maker, placed into it.

  The site's own intro animates exactly this, so match it: the ring draws, then the dot drops in.
- **Music:** low, steady pulse, minimal. One hit when the gap appears (0:27) and one when the dot lands (0:58).
- **Voice:** warm, unhurried, one person. About 150 words a minute.
- **Don't use:**
  - real film posters, stills, actor photos or studio logos (draw posters as abstract tiles; film titles as plain text are fine);
  - the Qloo logo, unless Qloo's brand kit allows it ("Powered by Qloo" as plain text is fine);
  - "hit", "will sell" or "guaranteed" (AYTO says "signal", "leans", "loved here").

## Notes for the animators

**What counts as fact.** All of these are real output and must stay exact.

**The London read (Qloo):**
- the lanes: Autobiographical (London #14), Indietronica (#13) and Tragedy (#15), none in the UK's top 30;
- 0 recent titles carry them;
- the look: Naturalistic +6, Intimate +6, Documentary-like +6, Gritty +4, less Cinematic and Polished;
- the controls: Glasgow, Liverpool and Leeds;
- the contrast city: Birmingham;
- the shoot base: Westminster (4 of the crowd's 8 places);
- the traced names: 27 of 27; with vs without Qloo, 13 of 13 against 0 of 10.

**The treatment (written by Claude Sonnet 5.5, copy it exactly):**
- **The story, simply** (167 words): "Amara Osei is 22 and lives in London. She wants to tell her own story out loud and in her own words, so she takes a small commission to record an audio memoir about her family. Her brother Kofi offers to help sort the tapes … The film ends with Kofi sitting down in front of the microphone."
- **Title:** Fourteen Minutes (also offered: The Long Way to Westminster, Say It Plain).
- **Amara Osei**, 22, café worker and first-time memoirist:
  - wants "to tell her own story in her own words";
  - hides that "she is afraid the truth will cost her the one family member she has left".
- **Beats:** The recorder goes on · Mismatched dates · Walking the river · The missing year · Quiet hour confrontation · Two voices.
- **Study: Queen & Slim.** Borrow: "Frame Amara's walking scenes in natural light with long takes, keeping the camera close to her face and letting ambient sound carry the mood."
- **Palette:** Thames Slate `#5b6770`, Wet Pavement `#3c4147`, Tape Hiss Amber `#b5833a`, Cafe Steam `#d9d4cc`, Bus Seat Moss `#4f5d4a`, Quiet Hour Brick `#8a5a48`.
- **Without Qloo**, the same model named *Mona Lisa* (1986), *Nil by Mouth* (1997) and *Top Boy*, and 0 of its 10 names trace.

**The UK compare-cities read:**
- Glasgow's widest open lane is Consequences of Crime (+26 over the UK and the other nine cities).
- London's are Autobiographical and Tragedy, and Liverpool's is the Entertainment Industry.
- Social Issues, Occult, Horror and Science Fiction lean high almost everywhere.

**The Paris drop:**
- the lanes Artistic Community, Avant-Garde Artists and Intellectual;
- the look: Creative, Minimalist Silhouettes, Neutral Palettes, Leather and Wool;
- the labels: Maison Kitsuné, Ami, A.P.C. and Acne Studios;
- the hero style: Salon Leather Jacket.

**Receipt chips.** In the app every name opens the Qloo request it came from. Draw a chip as a small rounded tag with a ticket icon. It's the visual proof of "traces to Qloo", so give it a moment of focus.

**Real screen recording, if you want an insert.** Record from the live URL, not localhost:
- The run page has three tabs: The brief, The evidence (Qloo) and Make it.
- Run the London showcase once beforehand so it opens instantly.
- Blur the poster wall behind the hero, because it shows real posters.

**Deliver:** an MP4 (H.264) at 1080p, uploaded to YouTube or Vimeo as public or unlisted. Devpost takes that link. Add English captions to the upload too.
