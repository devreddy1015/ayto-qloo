# AYTO: the YouTube video, drop-led cut (built)

A 1:41 demo that leads with a **streetwear drop in Los Angeles**, not the film. It's composition `AytoDrop` in
`video/` (`video/src/drop/`), 16:9, 1920×1080, with the shubh voice and burned-in captions. The film-led cut is
[YOUTUBE_SCRIPT.md](YOUTUBE_SCRIPT.md) (`AytoDemo`). Both share the look, the sound, the mark and the close.

**Why a drop:** LA is the obvious streetwear city. The non-obvious answer is the point: skate punk crossed with visual
arts and alternative dance, in silk and leather. It's also the format with the most to see: the look, materials, a
palette, styles on a mannequin in 3D, and a print drawn for them.

Every number below comes from the live run `r_ftChx5Q` on ayto-ocx1.onrender.com (31 Oct 2026, 06:00 IST), written
by Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5), 33 of 33 names traced. Don't round or change them.
