    How it works

    It reads the culture

    The agent finds your wave in Qloo, checks whether its popularity is rising or cooling over the last six months, and pulls the taste tags its fans share.

    It follows the fans to fashion

    In each country, it asks Qloo which fashion brands this wave's fans over-index on, then compares that with the country's usual favourites. Brands that only show up with the wave are the ones riding it.

    It measures the gap

    City by city, it compares how hot the wave is with how hot those brands are. Where the culture has landed and the fashion hasn't, there's room for a maker to get there first.

    Qloo describes aggregate taste and momentum across large groups of people. It doesn't know about any individual, and it isn't sales data. Treat every result as an early signal to test, not a forecast. Culture Gap never sends personal data to Qloo.
