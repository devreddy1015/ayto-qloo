      How it works

      Qloo thinks first

      The agent finds your wave in Qloo, reads the taste tags its fans share, who they are in aggregate, and, city by city, where the wave is hotter than the city's size predicts.

      It measures the gap

      It asks Qloo which fashion brands those fans favour and maps where that fashion has landed. Where the culture is ahead and the fashion isn't, there's room to get there first.

      Eleven agents build the kit

      Product line, collection, tech packs, costing, an evidence check, a with-and-without-Qloo diff, channels, name, launch plan, and a live drop page. Edit any stage and rerun from there.

      Qloo describes aggregate taste across large groups. It doesn't know about any individual, and it isn't sales data. Every brand, city and tag in a kit comes from a Qloo request you can open; the Test stage removes anything the agents made up. Culture Gap never sends personal data to Qloo.
