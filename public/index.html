      How it works

      Qloo reads the city

      AYTO finds your city in Qloo's taste graph and reads what its people love beyond the rest of the country, against a contrast city, with every request kept as a receipt.

      It finds the gap

      Flavours the city ranks far above the country, with too little recent supply, become lanes. Lane one is where the thing you're making is loved here and under-served.

      Your model writes into it

      A language model, the server's or your own, writes the film, the reel or the drop from that evidence only. A check removes any name it made up.

      Qloo describes aggregate taste across large groups, not individuals, and it isn't sales data. Every name comes from a Qloo request you can open; the Check stage removes anything the model made up. Your LLM key is used for this run only and never stored.
